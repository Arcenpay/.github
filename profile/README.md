## The infrastructure behind programmable commerce

ArcenPay is a modern billing and entitlement platform for organizations that
need more than payment acceptance. We connect the commercial relationship —
plans, payments, usage, access, and customer operations — in one coherent
platform.

Our platform gives teams the foundation to launch recurring services,
usage-based products, digital memberships, and machine-to-machine payment
experiences with confidence.

### Built for organizations that are moving commerce forward

- SaaS and API businesses
- Web3 applications and protocols
- Digital products and membership networks
- AI applications and autonomous agents
- Platforms embedding financial capabilities
- Enterprises modernizing their billing infrastructure

## Why ArcenPay

Modern products are no longer defined by a single checkout. They are ongoing
relationships shaped by subscriptions, usage, permissions, credits, and
software that can act on behalf of its users.

ArcenPay gives organizations one trusted layer for managing those relationships
across applications, wallets, platforms, and programmable payment networks.

| Business challenge | ArcenPay advantage |
| --- | --- |
| Disconnected billing and access systems | One coordinated platform |
| Complex recurring and usage-based products | Flexible commercial models |
| Manual entitlement updates | Access that follows the payment relationship |
| New payment and wallet experiences | Infrastructure designed for programmable commerce |
| Growing operational complexity | Clear controls, records, and workflows |

## Our perspective

The internet is moving from static purchases toward ongoing relationships:
subscriptions, usage-based services, digital access, and software that can act
on behalf of its users.

Traditional billing was designed for invoices and checkout pages. Blockchain
introduced new possibilities for ownership, portability, and programmable
payments, but using those possibilities in a real business still requires
simple billing, access control, customer management, and reliable operations.

ArcenPay exists to bring these pieces together — making advanced payment
infrastructure feel simple, dependable, and ready for production.

## The problem

Businesses often have to connect many separate systems to run a paid product:

- A payment processor
- A subscription manager
- An entitlement or feature-gating system
- Usage metering
- Invoices and credits
- Customer and account management
- Notifications and integrations

These disconnected systems make products expensive to build, difficult to
operate, and hard to adapt to new payment models.

Web3 and AI products face an additional challenge: payments and access must be
usable by wallets, protocols, applications, and autonomous software alike.

## The ArcenPay platform

ArcenPay provides a unified layer for:

- **Payments** — accept programmable and digital-asset payments
- **Subscriptions** — manage recurring plans and customer lifecycles
- **Usage** — support metered products and consumption-based pricing
- **Entitlements** — connect payment status to product access
- **Operations** — manage customers, invoices, credits, and notifications
- **Automation** — enable software and agents to pay for services

Our goal is to let product teams focus on their customers and products while
ArcenPay provides the operating layer behind billing and access.

## How it works

```text
Business creates a plan
            ↓
Customer or software chooses a service
            ↓
ArcenPay processes the payment relationship
            ↓
Access and usage are updated automatically
            ↓
The business manages everything from one platform
```

ArcenPay is designed to work across modern payment environments, including
wallet-based and blockchain-enabled experiences, while keeping the product
experience clear for businesses and their customers.
